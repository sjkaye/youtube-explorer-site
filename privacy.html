Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

YouTube Explorer is a personal dashboard that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google OAuth, it reads YouTube data for the channels the owner connects: channel and video details (YouTube Data API, youtube.readonly) and channel analytics and reports (YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs, yt-analytics.readonly). Access is read-only. It never uploads, edits, deletes, comments or posts.

How it uses the data

Only to show the owner dashboards and reports about their own channels. The data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

Where the data is kept

Everything, including the OAuth tokens, is stored on the owner's own computer and is not sent to any server operated by the app. The app is not reachable from the internet. When the owner asks for a written analysis, a summary of the relevant numbers may be processed by an AI assistant the owner has their own account with.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while the owner uses the app. Removing a channel in the app deletes its stored token. Access can also be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

YouTube Explorer uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through the GitHub profile that publishes this page.
