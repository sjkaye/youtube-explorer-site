YouTube Explorer

YouTube Explorer is a private, single-user dashboard that a YouTube creator runs on their own computer at home. It shows the analytics of the creator's own YouTube channels (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate) in more detail than YouTube Studio, alongside research on public YouTube channels and videos.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up or use it, and it is not reachable from the internet.

It asks Google for read-only access to YouTube data (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) for the channels its owner connects. It never uploads, edits, deletes or posts anything.
